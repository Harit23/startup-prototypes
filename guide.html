GROUPDRIVE

Buy together. Pay less.

People who want the same vehicle in the same city join one group. Dealers compete live for the whole group, and the lowest price wins.

Who uses it

  • Admin (laptop): sets up a group deal and watches the live bidding.
  • Dealers (phones): bid against each other to win the group.

How a deal runs

  1. The admin picks a vehicle: a car, bike, truck or bus (e.g. BMW M340i or an Ashok Leyland truck). They set the price, group size, city, payment terms and a timer, then press Start live bidding.
  2. Each dealer opens the Dealer page once and sets up their shop, e.g. BMW Dealer Satellite.
  3. The dealer taps Join bidding. Their first offer is ₹10,000 below the current lowest price.
  4. Dealers lower their price with −₹10,000 / −₹50,000 / −₹1,00,000, raise it back with the + buttons, or type a custom bid. Get out leaves the deal.
  5. The admin sees a live 1-2-3 leaderboard, the saving per vehicle and the total saving for the group.
  6. When the timer ends (or the admin closes it), the lowest price wins, and the group buys from that dealer.

How we earn

Main fee5% of the dealer's margin on every vehicle (about ₹30,500 per BMW M340i).
ExtraLoan and insurance partner commissions, and later demand data for dealers.

SITECREW

Amazon, but only for builders.

One place where builders buy everything a construction site needs, A to Z, from verified local sellers, and track every delivery.

Who uses it

  • Builders: search, compare sellers and order to their site.
  • Sellers (cement depots, brick makers, steel traders, electricians, truck and JCB owners, skilled crews): list products and handle orders from a phone.

How an order runs

  1. A seller picks their shop (or registers a new one) and adds products with price, unit and stock.
  2. A builder enters the company and site name, e.g. Shaligram Builders · Satellite Tower B.
  3. The builder searches or picks a category (cement, bricks, steel, sand, electrical, plumbing, tiles, paint, wood, machinery and trucks, skilled services) and compares prices from different sellers.
  4. They add items to the cart and press Place order to site.
  5. The seller sees the order arrive live, then taps Accept → Dispatched → Delivered.
  6. The builder watches each step update in My orders.

How we earn

Main fee5% commission on every order.
ExtraFeatured listings for sellers, a delivery fee, and later credit for builders through a finance partner.

How the demo works behind the scenes

  • It's a simple website hosted free on GitHub Pages. There's no login: you just pick your role.
  • Every phone and laptop shares one live online database, so a bid or order made on one device shows on the others within seconds.
  • To try it, open Admin (or Builder) on a laptop and Dealer (or Seller) on 2–3 phones at the same time.
  • If the internet fails, add ?local=1 to the link. It then runs offline in tabs on one device.
  • All prices and shops are sample data. Anyone with the link can add or change them, so don't enter real details.
  • It's a prototype to show the idea. Payments, verification and logins come later if we are selected.

Questions for the team to think about

  • GroupDrive: will dealers agree to give a 5% share of their margin? How do we collect each buyer's token deposit?
  • GroupDrive: which first segment is easiest to win: luxury cars, two-wheelers, or fleet trucks and buses?
  • SiteCrew: what quality checks do we need on sellers before builders trust them?
  • SiteCrew: should we start with one category (e.g. cement and steel) in Ahmedabad and then expand?
