PawPulse logo concepts

Six original marks for the PawPulse brand and the BioBand. Each shown on cream and deep green, at favicon sizes, as an app icon and in one colour.

Recommendation: 01 Paw + Pulse and 02 Band Ring. Paw + Pulse says pet care and live health in one glance and holds up at 16px, so it is the safest master logo. Band Ring ties directly to the BioBand product and works as its sub-brand lockup and clip-on hardware mark. Use 04 Capsule for engraving and 05 Monogram P as a favicon alternate.
01

Paw + Pulse

Hero, app icon
Mark at 64 / 32 / 16px
App icon (rounded square)
One colour, ink on mint

A clean paw whose central pad carries an ECG line: care and live data in one glance. Best as the hero logo and primary brand mark.

02

Band Ring

Hero, BioBand packaging
Mark at 64 / 32 / 16px
App icon (rounded square)
One colour, ink on mint

A collar loop with a clip, broken open by a heartbeat blip: it is literally the BioBand on a collar. Best for hero and the BioBand sub-brand.

03

Heart of Paws

App icon, social avatar
Mark at 64 / 32 / 16px
App icon (rounded square)
One colour, ink on mint

Four toe pads and one big pad assemble into a heart: the emotional bond. Best as a warm app icon and social avatar.

04

Band Capsule

Band hardware engraving
Mark at 64 / 32 / 16px
App icon (rounded square)
One colour, ink on mint

The BioBand seen side-on, with a glowing status dot and pulse slot. Minimal and product-like: ideal for hardware engraving.

05

Monogram P

App icon, favicon
Mark at 64 / 32 / 16px
App icon (rounded square)
One colour, ink on mint

A bold P whose stem runs out into a heartbeat tail, like a wagging tail. Strong app icon and favicon, also good on packaging.

06

Ears + Pulse

Hero, merch and stickers
Mark at 64 / 32 / 16px
App icon (rounded square)
One colour, ink on mint

A soft-tipped pair of ears, dog or cat, resting on a short pulse line. Friendly and ownable for hero and stickers.
