The band family

One band, a few ways to wear and use it. The mounts and modes below are concepts we want to test with pet parents.

BioBand Collar default

Clips onto the collar your pet already wears.

BioBand Harness mount concept prototype

For strong pullers and reactive dogs: sits on a harness, so there is no pressure on the neck. It tracks the dog; it does not treat or change behaviour.

BioBand Nudge mode concept prototype

For low-activity dogs: gentle activity nudges and a daily movement goal for the owner, shown with a paw-shaped light pattern. Not a medical tool.

How it works

Clip it on. The app does the rest.

The band collects. The PawPulse app keeps the records and turns them into care. No band yet? The app still works.

1

Clip it on

BioBand clips onto your pet's collar (Harness mount is a concept for dogs who pull). Switch it on and pair it in the app.

2

It syncs to the PawPulse app

Location and activity land in your pet's profile. Heart rate and temperature are proposed for later, subject to hardware validation.

3

Care follows

Medicines, routines and vet records sit beside the band's data, shared with family and your vet when you choose.

    Our promise

    The band is the star. Your records stay yours.

    The band's records live in the app, not in the band. The app works without the band, and a band is never a lock-in.

    Records stay yours

    Everything the band sends, and everything you add, sits in your pet's profile in the app.

    Always exportable

    You can export your pet's full history whenever you want (the export button in the demo is a placeholder, not built yet).

    The app outlives any band

    If a band stops working, the app keeps working and your history stays with you. You can also use the app with no band at all.

    Lesson from Whistle

    Whistle trackers were shut down on 31 Aug 2025 after an acquisition by Tractive. That is why PawPulse promises data export and an app that works without the band.

    What we will not claim

    PawPulse gives wellness trends, not medical diagnosis. We will not say a band can detect or treat disease or behaviour problems, and we will say so in the product too.
