How it works

1

Pick a vehicle

Admin chooses any car, bike, scooter, auto, tractor, truck or bus, plus group size and city.

2

Book your slot — ₹5,000

Buyers confirm with a ₹5,000 booking amount. It is adjusted against the final price, and refunded if the deal does not close.

3

Dealers bid down

Dealers on their phones keep lowering their price. Everyone sees the lowest bid live.

4

Group saves

Lowest bid wins when time is up. Every buyer in the group gets that price.
