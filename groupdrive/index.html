GroupDrive — Buy together. Pay less.

Buyers form a group for the same vehicle, and dealers compete in a live reverse auction. The lowest price wins.

I am the Admin I am a Dealer

How it works

1

Form a group

Admin picks a vehicle, group size, city and payment terms, then starts live bidding.

2

Dealers bid down

Dealers on their phones keep lowering their price. Everyone sees the lowest bid live.

3

Group saves

When time is up, the lowest bid wins. Every buyer in the group gets that price.
